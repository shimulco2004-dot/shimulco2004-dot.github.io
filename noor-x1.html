01 / THE BRIEF

Make AI disappear behind direction.

The story is simple: a woman leaves a contemporary Saudi villa, approaches a silent premium EV, enters the cabin, crosses the desert, reaches Riyadh, passes through a tunnel and arrives at the hero reveal. The hard part was keeping one believable driver, one believable vehicle and one believable world across every shot.

02 / ACTUAL REFERENCE LOCKS

Before motion, lock the world.

These are the actual generated reference sheets used for production. No redraws or placeholder boards: the face, wardrobe, vehicle geometry, cockpit, wheel design, headlamp signature, villa, desert road, Riyadh streets and tunnel were established before motion.

03 / FIRST FRAME → LAST FRAME

Direct between controlled endpoints.

Each video shot was generated between a controlled first and last frame using the locked references above. This reduced identity drift, vehicle morphing and random location changes.
